Södra Rörum · Hörby Municipality

Little Pinecone Lodge.

A charming red cottage tucked away in the peaceful forests of southern Sweden.

Sleeping arrangements
• Main cottage – Living-room sofa bed (double) + bunk-bed system (3 full-size beds)
• Garden annex – Two singles

Cottage details
47 m² main cottage with a warm, rustic feel and modern comforts. Fully equipped kitchen, cozy living area with fireplace, air-conditioning, washing machine and streaming services. Outside is a spacious 2 000 m² garden with an outdoor fireplace and play area. Two guests sleep in the small garden annex just a few steps from the main house.

Please note
Bed linen and towels are not included. Pillows and blankets are provided. If you would like to rent a complete set (towel + duvet cover + mattress sheet), the cost is 150 SEK per set. Please let us know a few days in advance. Payment is made in cash on arrival.

Final cleaning can be ordered for 1 500 SEK. If you choose not to order cleaning, we kindly ask that you leave the house in the same condition as when you arrived.

Feel free to use the indoor fireplace as much as you like — Note: please bring your own firewood (logs, kindling, etc.). The outdoor grill is also yours to enjoy! Note: please bring your own charcoal (and matches or lighter fluid if needed).

We want you to have the most relaxing and enjoyable stay possible. Happy fireside chats and delicious barbecues ahead! Let us know if you have any questions or need tips for the perfect fire or grill session Surrounded by towering trees, fresh air and the gentle sounds of nature, this is the perfect place to slow down and reconnect.
