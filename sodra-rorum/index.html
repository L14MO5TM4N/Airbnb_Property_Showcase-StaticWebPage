Södra Rörum · Hörby Municipality

Welcome to Little Pinecone Lodge.

A charming red cottage tucked away in the peaceful forests of southern Sweden. Surrounded by towering trees, fresh air and the gentle sounds of nature, this is the perfect place to slow down and reconnect.

Inside the main cottage you’ll find two warm and inviting rooms across 47 m² — ideal for curling up with a book or simply relaxing after a day exploring the Swedish countryside. Outside, a spacious 2 000 m² garden opens directly into the forest. Two of the guests sleep in the small garden annex, a cozy space just a few steps from the main house.

Sleeping arrangements
• Living-room sofa bed – converts into one full-size double bed (sleeps 2)
• Bunk-bed system – three full-size beds in total (sleeps 3)
• Garden annex – two full-size double beds (90 × 200 cm)

In total there are five full-size beds in the main house and two in the annex.

Please note
Bed linen (sheets, duvet covers, pillowcases) and towels are not included. If you would like us to provide a complete set, please reserve it at least 10 days before arrival. The cost is €20 per set (one set = linen for one bed + one bath towel).

The key to the annex is waiting for you in the key box. The grounds cover 2 000 m² and there is plenty of space to park several cars.

Feel free to use the indoor fireplace as much as you like — just bring your own firewood (logs, kindling, etc.). The outdoor grill is also yours to enjoy; please bring your own charcoal (and matches or lighter fluid if needed).

We want you to have the most relaxing and enjoyable stay possible. Happy fireside chats and delicious barbecues ahead! Let us know if you have any questions or need tips for the perfect fire or grill session.

Modern amenities
Guests can enjoy air-conditioning, a fully equipped kitchen, washing machine and streaming services. There is also an outdoor fireplace and outdoor play area.

Outdoor activities & Location
The property offers easy access to cycling, Mushroom picking and outdoor dining areas. Nearby attractions include Söderåsen National Park ↗ (38 km) and Elisefarm Golf Club ↗ (18 km). Malmö Airport is 60 km away.
