Södra Rörum, Cozy Forest Retreat - Pinecone Lodge

Welcome to Little Pinecone Lodge, your charming red cottage Located in the serene forests of Southern Sweden. Surrounded by towering trees, fresh air, and the soothing sounds of nature. Inside, you’ll find 2 warm and inviting rooms across 47 m². Curl up with a good book or simply relax after exploring the beautiful Swedish countryside. Outside, A big 2 000 m² garden opens into the forest. Two of the guests will sleep in the garden annex, a cozy space just steps from the main cottage.
