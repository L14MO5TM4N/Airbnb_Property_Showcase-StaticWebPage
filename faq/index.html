Frequently asked questions

Lakehouse with private pier (Bälinge)
Are bed linen and towels included?

No. Bed linen and towels are The property hasnot included. You can rent a set for 150 SEK per set. (paid in cash on arrival) Just ask a couple of days in advance.

Is parking available?

Yes. The property has free parking on the grounds.

Are life jackets available?

Yes, they are in a wardrobe in the cottage. By law you must wear a life jacket while using the rowboat.

Can we swim and fish?

Yes. Swimming and fishing from the pier or boat are possible. Adults need a fishing licence (fiskekort); children under 16 fish for free.

How far is the lake from the house?

The house sits about 100 metres from the lake, with trees in between, so there is no direct lake view from the windows.

Is cleaning Available?

Final cleaning can be ordered for 1 500 SEK. If you choose not to order cleaning, we kindly ask that you leave the house in the same condition as when you arrived.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome.

Do I need to bring firewood or charcoal?

Yes. You are welcome to use the indoor fireplace and outdoor grill, but please bring your own firewood and charcoal (plus kindling, matches or lighter fluid as needed).

Is self check-in available?

Yes, we offer self check-in.

Is the water safe to drink?

Yes, the tap water is fresh and safe to drink.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes, the cottage has good Wi-Fi (500 Mbps).
