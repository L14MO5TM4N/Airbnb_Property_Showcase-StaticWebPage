Welcome to Bälinge Lakehouse.

A charming 100-year-old cottage nestled by the serene waters of Bälinge lake. With its very own private pier, rowing boat and direct lake access, this is the perfect place for morning swims, peaceful fishing sessions or simply watching the sun rise over the water.

The house measures 86 m² and offers a warm blend of rustic charm and modern comforts. Inside you’ll find a fully equipped kitchen with a large dining table that seats up to 10 people, a cozy living area with fireplace, fast Wi-Fi (1 Gbit), air-conditioning and a TV. Outside, a spacious 1 400 m² garden invites you to relax in the hammock, play badminton or enjoy a barbecue.

Sleeping arrangements
• Main bedroom – one double bed + one bunk bed (ideal for two children or extra guests)
• Second bedroom – two single beds

Please note
Bed linen and towels are not included. Pillows and blankets are provided. If you would like to rent a complete set (towel + duvet cover + mattress sheet), the cost is 150 SEK per set. Please let us know a few days in advance. Payment is made in cash on arrival.

Final cleaning can be ordered for 1 500 SEK. If you choose not to order cleaning, we kindly ask that you leave the house in the same condition as when you arrived.

Feel free to use the indoor fireplace — some wood is available in the house, but for longer stays please bring or buy additional firewood. The outdoor grill is also at your disposal; simply bring your own charcoal.

Outdoor life & location
The private pier is perfect for swimming, sunbathing or enjoying a ride in the rowing boat (life jackets provided). The garden includes a children’s swing, hammock and badminton set. Indoor games are available for rainy days.

Nearby attractions include Söderåsen National Park ↗ (29 km), Kungsbygget Adventure Park ↗ (22 km) and Herrevadskloster ↗(27 km). Ängelholm-Helsingborg Airport is 45 km away. Across the lake you’ll find a small summer campground with a sandy beach, playground and kiosk.

The house sits about 100 metres from the lake, with trees in between, so there is no direct lake view from the windows. The three small red buildings you see in the photos all belong to the property (main house, storage and garage).

Whether you’re looking for quiet time to recharge, a fun family gathering or simply a well-earned break by the water, we hope you’ll feel right at home. We look forward to welcoming you to Bälinge Lakehouse.
