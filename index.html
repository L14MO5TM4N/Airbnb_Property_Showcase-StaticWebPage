Bälinge · Perstorp

Welcome to Bälinge Lakehouse.

Escape to our lake house nestled on the serene of Bälinge lake. Enjoy direct access to the lake with a private pier/dock that belongs to the house and a rowing boat, perfect for morning swims or fishing.

Fully equipped kitchen with a stove, oven, fridge & freezer microwave, dishwasher, coffee maker and kettle. Fast Wi-Fi, a fireplace for cozy evenings and a 1400 square meter garden, BBQ area. This historical home, over 100 years old, offers a perfect blend of rustic charm and modern comforts.

Söderåsens National Park is 29 km away, and Ängelholm-Helsingborg Airport is 45 km from the property. Water sports facilities are available, along with fishing, walking tours, bike tours, hiking, and diving.

Up to 4 guests · 2 bedrooms · 1 bathroom · Entire home
